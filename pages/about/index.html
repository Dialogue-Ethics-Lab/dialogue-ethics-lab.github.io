About the Dialogue Ethics Lab.

The Dialogue Ethics Lab is an educational hub that develops, researches, and facilitates learning around equity, justice-informed, and arts-based open-access resources for Israeli–Palestinian youth dialogue and shared-society programs.

Our collective brings together Palestinian and Israeli former youth participants and facilitators of shared society programs to reflect critically on dialogue practices and transform lived experience into tools, insights, and learning resources for the field.
